## Goal

## Bridge model and incremental regimes

One fixed, untrained PyTorch model exposes two registered causal nodes and a frozen output graph: a
query--key routing node and a source-value node feeding both attention transport and a residual
bypass. Source values contain known value-private, group-shared, and nuisance factors. Clean and
corrupt differ in registered routing, private, and shared causal factors **and** in independently
matched nuisance coordinates. The primary ground-truth controller patches only the clean routing,
private, and shared factors at their two factor nodes, retains corrupt nuisance state, and recomputes
every downstream path. It is therefore a selective multi-node intervention, not a sufficient-state
transplant. A complete two-node observed-state transplant and a full-output transplant are Tier-A
technical skylines only. Omission tests must independently break the routing path, private factor,
shared factor, attention-transport route, and bypass route while leaving both skylines operational.
The final logits come from a fixed readout after the registered residual/attention computation.

The scientific oracle is the **hybrid causal counterfactual**
`f(clean routing/private/shared factors, corrupt nuisance factors)`. It is computed independently from
the frozen factor graph and must preserve every corrupt nuisance byte. Selective recovery and
full-vocabulary restoration are defined against this hybrid—not against the observed clean output.
The observed clean state/output remains descriptive and is the donor for the two sufficient-state
skylines only. This prevents correct nuisance preservation from being misclassified as incomplete
recovery and prevents nuisance from becoming a cosmetic factor.

The generated panels contain six prespecified regimes, evaluated separately rather than pooled:

1. `ideal_hard`: hard registered routing, no bypass, no layer norm, orthogonal value factors, no
   noise. This is a bridge-local reference, not a v1.1 rerun.
2. `soft_attention`: finite-temperature softmax with every source receiving positive mass and the
   causal source neither one-hot nor uniform.
3. `residual_bypass`: soft attention plus a nonzero mean-source bypass and query residual.
4. `layernorm`: the preceding graph followed by frozen affine-free layer normalization.
5. `correlated_superposition`: correlated value-private and group-shared factors projected from an
   overcomplete latent space into a smaller residual space.
6. `realistic_noisy`: the full graph plus deterministic, seed-bound source noise at prespecified
   low/medium/high strata. Noise is part of the causal source state, not fresh live randomness.

Each development and confirmation panel has 24 independently seeded document-like blocks per regime
and eight paired rows per block (1,152 rows/panel). Regime, block, value identities, noise strata, and random seeds are
balanced across a fixed 16-value target set; development, confirmation, and method-train identifiers
are disjoint. Generated panels and all counterfactual donors are frozen before model inference.

The model must expose and test all registered paths. Soft regimes require strictly positive weights,
a causal attention weight in `[0.30, 0.90]`, and at least three noncausal weights above `0.01` per
row. Bypass and attention transport must each have nonzero clean--corrupt contribution. Layer norm
must be applied after their sum. The final two regimes must have latent dimension greater than
residual dimension, nonzero private/shared correlation, full-row-rank mixing, and all registered
noise strata represented. The attention bounds are construction checks chosen to exclude hard and
nearly uniform routing; they are locked before scientific generation and are not behavioral gates
tuned from panel outcomes.

## Bridge interventions, metrics, and gates

For each row, compute clean, corrupt, the selective factor-level ground-truth controller, a complete
observed-state transplant skyline, a complete output-vector skyline, a matched sham factor patch, a
matched-noncausal factor patch, and the following deliberately incomplete causal interventions:

- routing-only (private/shared values remain corrupt);
- value-private-only (routing and shared factor remain corrupt);
- value-shared-only (routing and private factor remain corrupt);
- routing-plus-private (shared factor omitted);
- routing-plus-shared (private factor omitted);
- attention-transport-only (the registered bypass route remains corrupt);
- bypass-only (the registered attention-transport route remains corrupt).

Every primary or incomplete intervention reruns downstream attention, residual mixing, layer norm,
and readout. The observed-state and output transplants are explicitly labeled sufficient-state and
estimator skylines and cannot satisfy the selective-controller gate. Donor deltas are rescaled only
for registered matched-strength negative-control comparisons; neither the factor-level ground truth
nor either skyline is rescaled.

The complete observed-state skyline replaces both registered routing/value observed states with their
clean donors, then recomputes the graph. The output skyline replaces the complete corrupt vocabulary
logit vector with the clean vector and performs no graph claim. Both must be byte-identical to their
clean donor endpoints. In contrast, the primary controller patches only the named causal factors and
must leave every corrupt nuisance factor byte-identical. A mutation that silently performs the
complete observed-state transplant must therefore fail the nuisance-preservation assertion even if
its output recovers perfectly.

Using hybrid-oracle-versus-corrupt target margin as the positive denominator, report block-bootstrap
intervals for:

- selective ground-truth recovery and full-vocabulary restoration relative to the hybrid oracle,
  plus both sufficient-state skyline checks;
- signed sham specificity and matched-noncausal margin;
- collateral error as non-target deviation from the nuisance-preserving hybrid oracle;
- necessity from registered routing-factor and value-factor erasures, individually and jointly;
- maximum incomplete-controller recovery and its gap from exact recovery;
- exact equality of the observed-state/output skylines to their donors and two-pass deterministic QA.

Eligibility is frozen before payload generation. Every row must have finite registered states,
weights, logits, norms, and derived values; hybrid-oracle target top-1; hybrid target margin `>1.0`;
corrupt target margin `<0.0`; hybrid--corrupt target effect `>1.0`; positive centered full-vocabulary scale
`>1e-4`; and positive output-skyline denominator `>1e-4`. Patch norms must be finite and nonzero.
No row failing finiteness may be excluded: it makes the regime technically invalid. Each regime
requires at least 185/192 common rows, at least 7/8 per independent block, and an exclusion rate at
most 4%; exclusion counts and causes are always reported.

All six regimes use the same prospective substantive gates and are evaluated in ladder order. Each
must satisfy: observed-state/output skyline equality; selective ground-truth recovery and
full-vocabulary restoration point `>=0.90` with lower CI `>0.85`; sham and noncausal margins point
`>=0.80` with lower CI `>0.70`; collateral ratio point `<=0.05` with upper CI `<0.10`; joint
necessity point `>=0.80` with lower CI `>0.70`; both routing-only and value-only necessity points
`>=0.20` with lower CIs `>0.10`; and ground-truth-minus-best-incomplete recovery gap point `>=0.15`
with lower CI `>0.10`. The first failed regime is terminal; later regimes are not scored and methods
remain unopened. Comparator direction, strict/inclusive boundaries, and anti-tautology mutations
that preserve both skylines while breaking each primary causal path are locked using independent
nonpanel fixtures before `GENERATOR_LOCK`.

## Separately frozen conditional method benchmark

The methods protocol is frozen before bridge inference and cannot change in response to bridge
metrics. It contains disjoint train (32 independent blocks x 16 rows = 512 rows), development (24 x
8 = 192), and confirmation (24 x 8 = 192) panels from `realistic_noisy` only. Train opens only after
a bridge-pass authorization. Train is the sole fit/feature-selection split; there is no hyperparameter
selection on development. Development and confirmation are a one-shot replication for the complete
registered method set, not a selected winner.

The target set has 16 values. Every train block contains every target once, so every target-specific
estimator has 32 rows in 32 independent blocks; the frozen minimum is 24 rows in 24 blocks. Development
and confirmation each balance every target over exactly 12 rows in 12 blocks. Insufficient target
support is a registered method failure: after common-cohort eligibility, every target must retain at
least 11 rows in 11 independent blocks. It never permits target pooling, exclusion, or reselection.

Methods are:

1. selective factor-node ground-truth controller, plus the observed-state transplant skyline;
2. every registered incomplete circuit subset (negative controls);
3. seeded random orthogonal rank-8 subspaces (`Q=qr(N(0,1))`, `P=QQ^T`) and PCA rank 8
   (`P=V_8 V_8^T` from centered train paired deltas);
4. paired difference-of-means: for each target value `c`, train-only
   `mu_c=mean(delta_i | target_i=c)`; the native row intervention is `mu_target`, while its matched
   version is rescaled to the row's ground-truth factor-delta norm;
5. LEACE-style concept extraction: with centered train state `X`, target one-hot `Z`,
   `C=Cov(X)+1e-4 I`, `W=C^(-1/2)`, and `Q=orth(W Cov(X,Z))`, define
   `E=I-C^(1/2) Q Q^T C^(-1/2)` and use the removed component `(I-E) delta`;
6. a standard single-branch TopK SAE with dictionary width `4d`, TopK 8, tied intervention site,
   2,000 Adam steps, learning rate `1e-3`, batch 128, and seeds `[1701,1702,1703]`. Reconstruction is
   fit label-free on the union of train clean/corrupt/sham observed states. After fitting, feature
   selection uses only train pairs: rank features by the mean absolute clean--corrupt code change and
   patch the top eight clean codes into the corrupt code before decoding;
7. a behavior-supervised low-rank linear controller trained against target recovery plus frozen
   collateral and sham penalties (skyline), with rank 8, 2,000 Adam steps, learning rate `5e-4`,
   loss `-recovery + 2*collateral_MSE + sham_recovery^2 + 1e-3*||delta||^2`, and seeds
   `[2701,2702,2703]`. It receives only train observed-state deltas and is differentiated through the
   frozen bridge graph.

For every ambient-state method, `delta` is the concatenation of the clean-minus-corrupt registered
routing and value observed states. A predicted delta is split back into those nodes, added to corrupt,
and followed by full graph recomputation. The matching sham substitutes the registered unrelated
donor delta but preserves the requested target label; the matched noncausal control uses a seeded
orthogonal delta at the same registered source node and through the same downstream pathways.
Necessity subtracts the same estimated causal component
from clean and recomputes the graph. Thus sufficiency, sham specificity, control margin, and necessity
all use the same fitted object rather than separately selected directions.

This is not K2. No method sees development or confirmation labels during fitting. Random subspaces
use neither pairs nor labels; PCA uses counterfactual pairs but no target labels; DiffMean/LEACE use
paired concept labels; SAE representation learning is label-free but its controller selection uses
paired differences; only the skyline optimizes behavior. This taxonomy is reported and prevents an
unsupported generic “supervision” claim. Native-strength and oracle-norm-matched estimands are distinct. The primary
cross-method frontier uses row-wise rescaling to the ground-truth controller L2 norm; native-strength
results remain separately reported. A zero native delta is scored as zero recovery and necessity and
is a method failure. Its matched-strength sensitivity is reported as mathematically undefined and
also fails the matched method gate; it never makes the row, target, method, or scientific endpoint
ineligible. A nonfinite delta is a method-level technical failure, not a dropped row.

Every learned seed is retained. Seed-specific estimates and an equal-weight descriptive mean across
the three seed estimates are reported. The authoritative learned-method rule requires **every one of
the three seeds** to pass every method gate independently on both development and confirmation. The
aggregate cannot rescue a failed seed. Failed seeds are not restarted or removed. Fit checkpoints,
fixed-step completion, and all losses are hash-bound.

The same common cohort and block-paired bootstrap report recovery, signed sham specificity,
matched-control margin, collateral damage, necessity, full-vocabulary restoration, and replication.
Method confirmation is reported for all registered methods. Both panels independently require at
least 185/192 rows, 7/8 per block, exclusion at most 4%, and the bridge behavioral denominators above.
A method is a joint-control pass only if its **matched-strength** estimand on both panels meets
recovery `>=0.70` (lower CI `>0.60`), signed sham specificity `>=0.50` (lower CI `>0.40`),
same-site matched-control margin `>=0.50` (lower CI `>0.40`), collateral
ratio `<=0.10` (upper CI `<0.15`), necessity `>=0.50` (lower CI `>0.40`), and full-vocabulary
restoration `>=0.70` (lower CI `>0.60`). Point comparisons are inclusive and interval comparisons
strict. Native metrics cannot substitute for a failed matched gate.

## Prospective interpretation

- Ground truth and the behavior-optimized controller pass while methods without a behavioral loss
  fail: behavioral-objective supervision helps under this fixed generator and controller-selection
  taxonomy. This does not identify semantic supervision generally.
- Ground truth passes and a simple projection matches complex methods: retain the simple method.
- Ground truth passes but all estimated methods fail: component discovery, not the evaluator, is the
  bottleneck.
- Ground truth fails after realistic operations: repair the intervention site/evaluator in a new
  version before judging methods; do not open methods v1 r2.
- The ground-truth controller works only in an earlier regime but not a later one: the first failed
  operation identifies where registered selective control ceased to transfer. Methods never open.
- Any bridge or method claim remains bounded to this synthetic transformer-like generator.

## Risks and one-way doors

- **Scientific payload opening is irreversible.** Durable access-may-have-occurred events are written
  and fsynced before the first path resolution/open; any ambiguous crash is terminal, never retried.
- **Conditional training is irreversible for confirmation status.** Methods data and optimizer are
  unreachable until the exact bridge hash authorizes them. Confirmation opens once for all methods.
- **A positive synthetic result can be overclaimed.** The paper/terminal schema hard-codes
  `natural_model_claim=false` and `k2_evaluated=false`; a post-result claim review is required before
  later publication use.
- **The evaluator and oracle could share a bug.** Use separate Torch and NumPy implementations plus
  analytic invariants and mutation tests. Co-location remains a disclosed limitation.
- **The bridge may be too easy despite added operations.** The incremental regimes and incomplete
  controls localize this; success is technical validation, not ecological realism.
- **GPU launch races.** A per-UUID atomic lock plus post-lock utilization recheck prevents knowingly
  sharing a busy device. No alternate-device retry occurs after an opening transition.

## Alternative considered and rejected

- **Another GPT-2 head search or natural prompt assay:** rejected as post-hoc selection and endpoint
  shopping after repeated support/specificity failures.
- **Immediately benchmark methods on v1.1:** rejected because exact one-hot routing and zero bypass
  make success too trivial to distinguish discovery from evaluator limitations.
- **One combined bridge/method namespace:** simpler operationally, but rejected because a failed
  positive control must leave training and method panels formally unopened.
- **Only add noise to v1.1:** rejected because it would violate the user's preservation instruction
