# PLAN — MSAE gen9 completion, exposed calibration, and independent-source handoff

Status: execution plan requested by the operator on 2026-09-14.

## Goal

Close the current MSAE workstream honestly and reproducibly by:

1. completing the reviewed gen9 control-plane sequence or recording the first
   terminal contract failure and rejecting gen9;
2. if and only if every gen9 gate passes, launching v3 as an
   **exposed-source calibration replay**, recording verified live handoff, and
   returning immediately without waiting for its result;
3. acquiring one prospectively chosen, genuinely new source, checking its
   licensing, task support, accessible-history overlap, and provenance, and
   creating a replacement blind payload without neural/model scoring;
4. preserving the prohibition on K2 or branch training until that independent
   source passes its prescore gate;
5. integrating the August SAE-basis-selection and bridge-v2 evidence into the
   manuscript and claim ledger;
6. updating `RESULTS.md`, `ANALYSIS.md`, `TODO.md`, and readiness documentation;
   and
7. committing the resulting work as small logical conventional commits after
   verification, claim review, diff review, and a staged secret scan.

## Non-goals

- Do not call v3 independent, pristine, confirmation, final-test, or Stage C.
- Do not open or content-read the quarantined Atlas final files.
- Do not alter a create-once gen9 artifact after publication or continue past a
  negative gate. A negative containment/capability/review/prescore result
  rejects gen9 and ends its one-way sequence.
- Do not run K2/MSAE/branch training.
- Do not score the new independent source with a model in this task. The task
  ends at a frozen, history-screened, blind replacement payload and explicit
  readiness/stop status.
- Do not promote basis-selection v1 or bridge v2 beyond the existing
  post-result claim review.
- Do not push commits.

## Constraints and evidence policy

- The frozen gen9 authority remains
  `docs/plan-msae-independent-measurement-v3-post-m9-gen9.md` and its exact
  independent `VERDICT: SHIP` review. This umbrella plan cannot weaken or
  reorder that plan's gates.
- Gen9 actions follow the one-way order at gen9-plan lines 381–402. Safe tests
  exclude the registered real-tmux node until changed-region publication and a
  pre-containment review exist.
- The existing v3 source is exposed AMALGUM and remains only a calibration
  replay. Confirmation scoring and Stage C stay unreachable.
- No source bytes are acquired until a dedicated v4 source/payload contract is
  independently reviewed. That contract must freeze the upstream identity,
  ordered candidate/fallback policy, whole-corpus inclusion/exclusion rule,
  natural resampling unit, task-support minima, overlap algorithm/thresholds,
  deterministic split algorithm/seed, payload schema, custody, and exposure
  tripwires. Candidate failure is retained; substitution is permitted only by
  the prospectively reviewed ordered fallback policy.
- The first prospective candidate is the official Universal Dependencies
  English-Atis treebank. Its natural unit is one source utterance/sentence; no
  claim of document- or speaker-level independence is permitted. It does not
  provide a multi-genre or named-entity benchmark, so those endpoints must be
  marked not applicable/limited rather than synthesized.
- Source acquisition and screening may use text and labels from the new source;
  the replacement blind evaluation payload is generated by a non-reporting
  deterministic command and is thereafter inspected by metadata/hash only.
- No model scoring occurs until the new source's filename/source-family check,
  task-support audit, full accessible-history overlap audit, and external
  prescore review pass.
- Existing tracked and untracked artifacts are evidence. Consolidation means
  publish a hashed path inventory assigning every porcelain path to a logical
  commit, `.gitignore`, or a justified local-only retained-data class; never
  delete unexplained results or stage caches, model weights, private keys,
  sockets, large transient outputs, or quarantined payload contents. Completion
  requires zero unexplained porcelain paths.

## Grounding and low-risk assumptions

- The deterministic query backend is unavailable from this undeclared nested
  repository, matching the documented v3 limitation. Direct inspection found
  the gen9 dispatch surface in
  `scripts/msae_independent_measurement_v3_post_m2_gen9_runtime.py`, the seven
  prospective gen9 files, the gen9 CPU suite, and the frozen one-way sequence.
- Repository inspection found no active tmux session and no active compute
  process. Hardware availability is not authorization; only `launch-gen9` may
  select/query a GPU after signing.
- “Obtain a genuinely independent source” is interpreted as acquire, pin,
  license, validate, overlap-screen, split, and seal a replacement payload—not
  produce an independent neural result. This is the most conservative reading
  consistent with the user's explicit instruction not to score before history
  support and overlap checks.

## Approach

First finish the existing gen9 gates exactly, generating external review
transcripts at each required boundary. Only a fully passing sequence can create
M3/M4, prescore, authorization, and a calibration launch. Invoke the launcher
and wait only for its verified live-handoff return, as the
frozen protocol requires. The persistent monitor alone may later publish a
terminal result; observing that result is outside this task unless separately
authorized by a successor audit plan.

After the gen9 launch or terminal stop, create a separate v4-style
independent-source namespace. First freeze and review its exact source/payload
contract; then pin the selected official upstream source and
license; record a content manifest; compute label/support and accessible-history
overlap before any neural operation; then deterministically split source units
and create a sealed replacement payload. Publish only manifests, support,
overlap, and hashes. The private payload itself must be ignored by Git and
content-unopened after sealing.

Finally revise narrative artifacts so they distinguish: (a) the main
SAE-capacity R2 result, (b) the non-promoting August successor results, (c) the
   v3 verified exposed-calibration live handoff or upstream stop, and (d)
   independent-source readiness without a model result. A later terminal
   calibration result remains deferred to a separately reviewed successor audit.

### Alternatives considered

- **Reuse AMALGUM as confirmation.** Rejected because its source and former
  blind payload are exposed.
- **Choose among several corpora after measuring overlap.** Rejected as an
  outcome-blindness and researcher-degrees-of-freedom problem. English-Atis is
  selected prospectively; failure is retained rather than replaced ad hoc.
- **Read the old blind files to clone their schema.** Rejected. Public builders,
  public task files, and documented schemas are the only permissible guides.
- **Restart K2 while the source pipeline runs.** Rejected by the scientific stop
  rule and the user's instruction.
- **Commit all untracked entries wholesale.** Rejected. Artifacts are grouped
  by lineage and deliberately staged; transient or private objects remain out.

## Risks and one-way doors

- Gen9 reviews, manifests, containment, capability, M3/M4, signature, nonce, and
  run artifacts are create-once; failure terminates the generation.
- Real tmux containment creates processes and sockets. Use only the registered
  command and verify complete extinction.
- Signing consumes a one-use authorization path; launching consumes its nonce.
- A blind payload loses scientific value if its contents appear in command
  output, review context, Git, logs, or later inspection. Only the builder may
  write it; later operations are metadata/hash-only.
- Upstream datasets can have restrictive licenses. Record and comply with the
  exact license; do not commit redistributed text unless permitted and needed.
- Large untracked artifacts can make an accidental broad stage destructive or
  impractical. Stage explicit paths only and inspect object sizes before commit.
