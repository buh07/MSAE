## Receipt trust, explicitly not self-certification

Visible pairs and freshly observed hashes are never expected receipts. Consumers
require exact outside expectations **and** a containing approved control/manifest.
Ordinary code/config/review files remain nlink1; pairing all history is prohibited.

The intended non-circular production chain is:

1. An operator-provided, independently approved exact-byte whole/storage binding
   anchors ordinary implementation/config/review inputs. The implementation lists
   review paths, not its own future review hash. Report path or first line alone
   cannot establish authority.
2. Outside authority review binds exact baseline/history/authority pair receipts,
   hashes, schemas and lineage after publication. Its independently supplied
   digest is the trust root; authority does not embed its own unknown receipt/hash.
3. Within a successful live producer, retained handles/returned receipts bind
   entry/ready/started controls to the anchored authority and acquisition lineage.
   Fresh-process recovery cannot invent those expectations from the namespace:
   it needs a separately authenticated outside receipt catalog, followed by full
   canonical control/schema/lineage/evidence validation.
4. An independently pinned readiness approval binds terminal/seal/role receipts
   before any scorer capability. C1 and C2 remain separately gated.

**The outside production binding, canonical control readers and full trust chain
are not implemented/approved yet.** `recover_current_custody` validates only
current opaque pairs against a caller's externally approved catalog digest; it
never authorizes source work or infers historical producer success. This boundary
is intentional, not completion of P3/P5. An untrusted caller-supplied digest is not
an independent approval. No self-control payload is used as its own receipt root.
