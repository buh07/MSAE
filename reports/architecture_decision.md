# Atlas-v1 Architecture and Training Decision

**Decision: equivocal / no-decision. Do not start new model training now.**

This is a stop decision, not a claim that learned separation is impossible. G1 and G2 both lack mandatory inferential/collateral evidence. Under the preregistered precedence rule, missing primary evidence forces equivocal and forbids training authorization.

The weaker fixed-probe diagnostics are scientifically useful:

1. L3/L4 fixed-probe point estimates show a strong lexical/semantic-vs-positional-complement contrast.
2. They do not selectively segregate absolute from structural position: both split and broad positional margins are near zero or negative because of cross-decoding.
3. Every existing K=2 checkpoint has worse point macro-selectivity than the frozen simple projection comparator, with particularly poor positional localization.
4. The simple projection is a promising next comparator, not a winner: mandatory collateral selection and simultaneous equivalence bounds are missing.
5. Branch-removal CE damage is large, but off-manifold damage cannot establish family-specific causality.

Therefore a new K>2, PCC/shared, dense-residual, or replacement MSAE training run is **not warranted by current evidence**. The next work, if the project continues, is analysis completion: production refit inference, Tier-2 collateral, stability, and valid matched-random/sham counterfactuals. Only a later valid G1/G2 decision may authorize a training preregistration.

The blind final-test partition remains locked and unopened. This interim stop is not a final G3 paper-branch selection and does not authorize M8.
